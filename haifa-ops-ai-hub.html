Governance & Management Direction
Open decisions requiring leadership input — things blocking further progress or scale.
Enterprise System Access for AI
AI tools need to connect to Windchill and SAP. Access rights differ between users. No framework exists for how AI should securely access enterprise data.
Who owns the framework for AI-to-enterprise integration?
AI Budget Ownership
Token-based tools (Claude, ChatGPT) scale with usage. Costs can surprise. Uber burned its entire 2026 AI budget in 4 months. Currently unclear who controls Haifa spend.
Is there a site-level AI budget? Who controls token spend?
Next Pain Points for AI
We have 9 active use cases proving value. We need management to identify the next 2-3 highest-value operational pain points where AI should focus next.
What are the top 3 processes costing you the most time or money?
AI Validation & Sign-off
When AI generates solutions — who validates the output? Who maintains it over time? Critical for regulated operations (FDA, ISO 13485).
Who signs off on AI-generated work entering production workflows?
Approval Process — Changed Aug 2026
Access to ChatGPT Enterprise and Claude Code now requires line manager approval (not AI Champions). Managers evaluate business value before granting access.
Managers: are you aware this is now your responsibility?
